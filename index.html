Hydrogeology

Groundwater Level Modelling & Land Use — Khulna

Interpolated depth to groundwater for 2000, 2010 and 2015 from 42 DPHE wells (IDW tuned by cross-validation) and tested its relationship with land use.

  • Average decline of 6.6 m from 2000 to 2015 (≈0.44 m/yr)
  • 81% of the area declined by more than 4 m
  • Weak link with built-up land (r = 0.28), pointing to regional over-abstraction
