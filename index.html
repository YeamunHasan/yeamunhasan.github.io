About Me

I am an M.Sc. student in Remote Sensing and GIS at Jahangirnagar University, with a B.Sc. in Geography and Environmental Studies from the University of Rajshahi. I work with SAR and optical satellite data to study floods, land-use change and household flood vulnerability in Bangladesh, and have published two papers in the Bangladesh Maritime Journal. Alongside research at the Cox's Bazar Development Authority, I teach GIS to over 200 students at StatX Academy and mentor OpenStreetMap contributors as an Open Mapping Guru with the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team. I won First Prize in the poster competition at State of the Map Asia 2024.

Hydrogeology

Groundwater Level Modelling & Land Use — Khulna

Interpolated depth to groundwater for 2000, 2010 and 2015 from 42 DPHE wells (IDW tuned by cross-validation) and tested its relationship with land use.

  • Average decline of 6.6 m from 2000 to 2015 (≈0.44 m/yr)
  • 81% of the area declined by more than 4 m
  • Weak link with built-up land (r = 0.28), pointing to regional over-abstraction
